# Fact sheet: Brooklyn Org, intergroup tensions, care resources, parks

Researched Nov 21 2026. ⚠ marks uncertain items; park coordinates are approximate.

TL;DR: Most facts are confirmed with sources. The uncertain ones are flagged ⚠. The parks coordinates are my own approximations and were not checked against a source, so verify them before placing anything.

## A. Brooklyn Org
- It is Brooklyn's public community foundation, founded in 2009 as Brooklyn Community Foundation. It renamed itself "Brooklyn Org" in October 2023, announced at its Changemakers Ball. The CEO is Jocelynne Rainey. https://brooklyn.org/article/brooklyn-community-foundation-is-now-brooklyn-org/
- Office: 80 Hanson Place, 5th Fl, Brooklyn NY 11217, at South Portland Ave in Fort Greene. ⚠ LinkedIn and ZoomInfo list 1000 Dean St Ste 307, but the official contact page still says Hanson Place. https://brooklyn.org/contact/
- Grantmaking grew from $7.4M (2019) to $19.5M (2025). Over the same period assets grew from $74.5M to $146.8M and the number of donor-advised funds doubled. https://brooklyn.org/article/chronicle-of-philanthropy-stop-chasing-younger-donors-start-inviting-them-in/
- 2024 split: more than $3.8M in community (participatory) grants and more than $15.7M through donor-advised funds. This shows its role as a bridge between donors and nonprofits. https://www.bkreader.com/non-profit-community/brooklyn-org-awards-15m-to-local-nonprofits-battling-food-insecurity-funding-cuts-and-rising-costs-11592865
- How its grants are decided: since 2021, all strategic grantmaking is participatory. Residents on an advisory council of about 12 pick the grantees. A typical grant is $45K a year for 3 years of general operating support. https://brooklyn.org/article/three-lessons-learned-from-a-holistic-shift-to-participatory-grantmaking/
- Programs over time: Neighborhood Strength launched in 2015. The Brooklyn Elders Fund became the first participatory fund in 2019. Funds also covered youth, immigrant rights and wellness/recovery. ⚠ These now look folded into impact areas (Older Adults, Youth, Immigrants and others). https://www.insidephilanthropy.com/home/2022-9-12-how-the-brooklyn-community-foundation-brings-grantmaking-to-the-people
- Newer programs: the January 2026 white paper names a "Neighborhood Collective" (place-based investments) and a Microgrant program. https://brooklyn.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Brooklyn-Org-White-Paper-The-Foundation-Of-Our-Future_January-2026.pdf
- Spark Prize: started in 2016 and goes to nonprofits advancing racial justice and equity. Each winner gets $100K with no strings attached. A committee of residents narrows the field to 20 finalists, then picks 5 after interviews. It has awarded more than $5.4M in total. https://brooklyn.org/sparkprize/
- 2026 winners: Asiyah Women's Center, Black Trans Femmes in the Arts, S.T.O.P. (Surveillance Technology Oversight Project), The B.R.O. Experience Foundation and YVote. They were honored March 3, 2026 at Barclays Center. https://brooklyn.org/article/brooklyn-org-reveals-winners-of-the-2026-spark-prize-honoring-five-trailblazing-nonprofits/
- 2025 winners: Brooklyn Book Bodega, Brownsville Community Culinary Center, CHiPS, Good Call and TechFIN. https://brooklyn.org/article/2025-brooklyn-org-spark-prize-honors-five-trailblazing-nonprofits/

## B. Intergroup tensions
1. **Williamsburg: Satmar Hasidim vs. cyclists and newcomers.** In December 2009 the city's transportation department (DOT) removed 14 blocks of the Bedford Ave bike lane between Flushing Ave and Division St. Activists repainted it, and there was a "funeral" ride and a topless ride. In 2025 Mayor Adams ordered part of the 2024 Bed-Stuy protected lane removed after Hasidic complaints. I found no mediation effort. https://nyc.streetsblog.org/2009/12/01/dot-sandblasts-14-blocks-of-bike-lane-off-bedford-avenue and https://gothamist.com/news/adams-orders-removal-of-bike-lane-protection-along-stretch-of-brooklyns-bedford-avenue/
2. **Williamsburg/Bed-Stuy: the Broadway Triangle.** This is a better mediation case than Los Sures. A coalition of Black and Latino groups sued in 2009, saying the city's housing plan favored Hasidic families. It settled in December 2017: more than 375 affordable units, a broader mix of apartment sizes, and $2.4–4.8M for fair-housing education. Satmar factions split over the deal. https://www.nyclu.org/press-release/settlement-ends-discriminatory-development-key-affordable-housing-site-brooklyn
3. **Sunset Park: Industry City rezoning.** The developers withdrew the plan on September 22, 2020, after UPROSE (a largely Latino group), Protect Sunset Park, CM Menchaca and four members of Congress opposed it. ⚠ I found no sourcing on Chinese residents' positions. https://www.brooklynpaper.com/industry-city-withdraws-rezoning-application/
4. **Sheepshead Bay: the Voorhies Ave mosque, 2009–2011.** This replaces the Bay Ridge case, which I couldn't source. Many opponents were Russian-Jewish immigrants. Their group, Bay People Inc., held rallies with "Islam not welcome here" signs, and a 2010 counter-protest followed. The courts dismissed their suits in 2011, and CB15's chair pushed back on the race-baiting. ⚠ I found no source for a Bay Ridge "Little Palestine" co-naming. The nickname refers to 5th Ave, not Bay Ridge Ave. https://www.brooklynpaper.com/storobin-joins-quixotic-fight-against-sheepshead-bay-mosque/
5. **Brighton Beach: Russian- and Ukrainian-speaking residents after 2022.** Tension showed mostly in symbols. A "Taste of Russia" shop was renamed "International Food," and a group held "Russians for Ukraine" rallies. Sources describe the community as divided but without open conflict, and a 2025 fundraiser drew both groups. ⚠ I didn't check Central Asian newcomers. https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2022/3/30/whats-in-a-name-russians-in-new-york-adapt-amid-ukraine-war
6. **Midwood: Coney Island Ave "Little Pakistan" and Orthodox Jewish neighbors.** It was long cited as a model of coexistence: COPO ran a basketball league with Orthodox Jewish youth. A later account describes the two groups as living "separately but side by side." ⚠ Most sources are from 2004–2007. https://www.salon.com/2004/06/22/heroes_copo/ and https://forward.com/news/8388/jewish-muslim-incident-rocks-ethnic-balance/
7. **Flatbush/East Flatbush: Caribbean renters vs. speculators.** The Flatbush Tenant Coalition (part of Flatbush Development Corp) and Equality for Flatbush organize tenants. HUD says rising rents are displacing Black residents. The Caton Market vendors returned to the new Flatbush Central development in 2022. https://www.huduser.gov/portal/casestudies/study-051524.html
8. **Bushwick: Latino renters and the rezoning.** The Bushwick Community Plan (2018) was co-written by CB4, Make the Road NY, Churches United for Fair Housing, Brooklyn Legal Services Corp. A and RiseBoro. The city then broke from it, and the group Mi Casa opposed any rezoning. https://gothamist.com/news/newly-unveiled-bushwick-community-plan-grapples-with-gentrification-in-a-rapidly-changing-neighborhood
9. **Floyd Bennett Field shelter.** This 2,000-bed tent shelter in Marine Park opened in fall 2023 and closed in January 2025. Critics included advocates (cold, remote) and bipartisan opponents. https://www.brooklynpaper.com/floyd-bennett-field-shelter-shut-down/

## C. Unhoused people and mental health
- **Right to shelter:** The Callahan decree (1981) still stands. The 2024 settlement allows 30-day limits for most single adult migrants during the emergency. In January 2026, Mamdani ordered emergency migrant shelters phased out. ⚠ I couldn't confirm whether the 30-day rule has formally ended. https://www.coalitionforthehomeless.org/callahan-settlement-preserves-the-right-to-shelter-while-implementing-temporary-measures-during-the-humanitarian-crisis/
- **Street outreach:** Call 311 and ask for street outreach. Breaking Ground runs the city's 24/7 outreach contract for all of Brooklyn. https://www.breakingground.org/what-we-do
- **Safe Havens:** Low-barrier beds with no curfew. Referral comes only through outreach teams, and usually needs about 6 months of documented street homelessness. There were roughly 3,061 beds after the Adams expansion. https://www.nyc.gov/site/dhs/outreach/frequently-asked-questions.page
- **Drop-in center:** CAMBA's The Gathering Place, 2402 Atlantic Ave (Broadway Junction), open 24/7 with meals, showers and case management. https://camba.org/career/supervisor-i-the-gathering-place-drop-in-center/
- **B-HEARD:** Mental-health teams sent through 911. Hours are 9am–1am in 31 of the city's 78 precincts. Brooklyn coverage: East New York, Brownsville, East Flatbush, Canarsie, Crown Heights, Prospect Lefferts and others (precincts 63, 67, 69, 71, 73, 75). Expansion is paused. ⚠ The program is moving back to FDNY in 2026. https://mentalhealth.cityofnewyork.us/b-heard
- **988:** Call or text 988, free and 24/7. "NYC 988" replaced NYC Well. You can also chat at nyc988.cityofnewyork.us, and it doesn't ask about immigration status. https://access.nyc.gov/programs/nyc-988/
- **Coalition for the Homeless:** Crisis Intervention at 129 Fulton St, Manhattan, walk-ins Monday–Friday from about 9am. Hotline 888-358-2384. https://www.coalitionforthehomeless.org/resources/coalition-for-the-homeless-crisis-intervention-department/
- **Talking with dignity:** Say "person experiencing homelessness." Ask "What do you need?" instead of assuming. Ask easy-to-decline questions, don't touch belongings, and never press for someone's story. https://housingplusnyc.org/announcements/centering-the-person/ and https://unitedwaymassbay.org/blog/allyship-101-how-to-support-people-experiencing-homelessness

## D. Parks (coordinates ⚠ approximate, not sourced)
- **Lena Horne Bandshell:** 141 Prospect Park West, entered near the 9th St and 11th St park entrances. Built 1939–40 as a WPA project and renamed for Horne in 2021. About 40.6640, -73.9774. https://bricartsmedia.org/celebrate-brooklyn/directions/
- **BRIC Celebrate Brooklyn! 2026:** June 4 to September 19, 15 free shows plus 3 paid benefit concerts. The season is over. https://bricartsmedia.org/about/news/press-release/bric-celebrate-brooklyn-returns-with-radical-joy/
- **Prospect Park, other spots** (no source checked): Grand Army Plaza about 40.6743, -73.9701; Long Meadow about 40.668, -73.971; Boathouse about 40.6608, -73.9644; Lake about 40.656, -73.969.
- **Green-Wood:** Main gate at 5th Ave and 25th St, Gothic Revival brownstone completed 1861. ⚠ It's credited to Richard Upjohn & Son, and sources split between father and son. Monk parakeets have nested in its spires since the 1970s. Battle Hill (about 216 ft) is Brooklyn's highest point, with the Minerva statue saluting the Statue of Liberty. The cemetery covers 478 acres. Gate about 40.6587, -73.9942; Battle Hill about 40.6538, -73.9926. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green-Wood_Cemetery
- **Green-Wood burials:** Basquiat and Bernstein, plus Tiffany, Greeley and Boss Tweed. https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/you-could-be-first-artist-residence-historic-green-wood-cemetery-180975377/
- **Brooklyn Bridge Park:** 85 acres along 1.3 miles of waterfront, from just past the Manhattan Bridge in Dumbo to Atlantic Ave. Jane's Carousel (1922) is in the Empire Fulton Ferry section, not at Pier 2. Pier 1 has lawns and a playground, Pier 2 has sports courts and swings, Pier 3 a labyrinth, Pier 4 a beach, Pier 5 sports fields, and Pier 6 the playgrounds (Slide Mountain, Water Lab, Swing Valley). The Squibb Park Bridge (2013) links Brooklyn Heights to Pier 1. Carousel about 40.7045, -73.9915; Pier 6 about 40.6929, -74.0010. https://brooklynbridgepark.org/places-to-see/
- **Brooklyn Botanic Garden:** 52 acres, founded 1910. ⚠ The address (990 Washington Ave) is from my own knowledge; no source confirmed it. There's also an Eastern Parkway entrance. The Japanese Hill-and-Pond Garden (Takeo Shiota) was built 1914 and opened 1915. The Steinhardt Conservatory dates to 1988. The Cherry Esplanade hosts Sakura Matsuri. Admission: adults $22, under-12 free, some free Community Tickets each day, and pay-what-you-wish Tuesday–Friday, November–February. About 40.6694, -73.9624. https://www.bbg.org/visit/hours
