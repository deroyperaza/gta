# Fact sheet: living Brooklyn politician cameos

Researched Nov 23 2026.

All six still hold office, and I checked each title against 2026 sources. Big change: the Nov 3 2026 midterms flipped both chambers, so Jeffries is Speaker-designate and Schumer is fighting to become Majority Leader. Both switches happen in January, so today their titles are still "Democratic Leader." Every quote below is something I saw on the page I cite unless I mark it otherwise. All lat/lng are my own approximations; check each pin on a map. Every spot is an office, a school or a public plaza, never a home.

Pin clustering: James (55 Hanson Pl) and Williams (Brooklyn Tech) are about 400 m apart in Fort Greene. Jeffries (Midwood HS) and Schumer (Madison HS) are both on Bedford Ave, about 2.7 km apart.

**Shahana Hanif: OK, with care**
- **Title:** NYC Council Member, District 39 (Democrat), second term 2026–2029. Chairs the Committee on Disabilities.
- **Tie:** Born and raised in Kensington to Bangladeshi immigrant parents. Her district covers Kensington, Windsor Terrace, Park Slope, Gowanus, Carroll Gardens, Cobble Hill, the Columbia Waterfront and parts of Boerum Hill and Borough Park.
- **Spot:** Her district office, 197 Bond St, Gowanus (about 40.6822, -73.9870).
- **Facts:** She is the first Bangladeshi and first Muslim woman elected to the City Council, and the first woman to hold District 39. Before office she ran Participatory Budgeting for Council Member Brad Lander. She was lead sponsor of the NYC Abortion Rights Act and Universal Curbside Composting. A lupus diagnosis at 17 pushed her into public service.
- **Quote:** "In 2026, my goals are to make Brooklyn more affordable, accessible, and safe for everyone." (PoliticsNY Q&A, Mar 16 2026)
- **Quote:** "Our office helps connect constituents to city services, for issues big and small." (same Q&A)
- **Pocket Civics:** A Council Member is one of 51 people who write and vote on the city's laws and its budget. Their district office is where you go when the city isn't picking up the trash or fixing the heat.
- **Flag:** Her Gaza stance is a live fight in her district. She was arrested at a 2023 ceasefire rally, and a 2021 retweet she deleted drew criticism. In May 2026 Council Member Inna Vernikov attacked a "Jahannam" post of hers; I saw that only in a search summary. In 2026 she joined DSA's elected-officials bloc. Keep her to composting and city services.

**Jumaane Williams: OK**
- **Title:** NYC Public Advocate, in office since 2019. He won a new term on Nov 4 2025 with 72.9% and was sworn in at the start of 2026.
- **Tie:** Flatbush and East Flatbush. He held Council District 45 from 2010 to 2019 and started out as an organizer at the Greater Flatbush Beacon School.
- **Spot:** Brooklyn Technical High School, 29 Fort Greene Pl, his high school (about 40.6890, -73.9765). The Public Advocate's office itself is in Manhattan (1 Centre St), so I didn't use it. Brooklyn College, where he earned his BA and MA, is the alternative, but it sits right next to Jeffries' Midwood HS pin.
- **Facts:** He is the son of Grenadian immigrants and was described in 2009 as the first Grenadian-American elected in New York. On the Council he sponsored the Community Safety Act (2013), which created an NYPD Inspector General and passed over Mayor Bloomberg's veto, and the Fair Chance Act ("Ban the Box," 2015). He speaks openly about having Tourette's and ADHD.
- **Quote:** "The public advocate also serves as an ombudsman for city government, providing oversight for city agencies." (PoliticsNY Q&A, Aug 27 2026)
- **Quote:** "I am a first-generation Brooklynite of Grenadian heritage." (same Q&A)
- **Pocket Civics:** The Public Advocate is the city's elected watchdog. They take your complaint when a city agency fails you, can introduce bills in the Council and are first in line to fill in as mayor if the mayor leaves office.
- **Flag:** The Tourette's is his to talk about. Never play it for a gag. He was arrested at protests (2011, 2018), and a 2009 arrest after a domestic dispute ended with the charges dropped. He backs Hochul for governor in 2026 after running against her twice. None of this matters for a light cameo.

**Chi Ossé: CAUTION**
- **Title:** NYC Council Member, District 36 (Democrat), second term 2026–2029. Co-chair of the Council's LGBTQIA+ Caucus since Feb 9 2026, and co-chair of the Brooklyn Delegation.
- **Tie:** Bed-Stuy and North Crown Heights. His Haitian-American family has lived in Brooklyn for three generations. He went to PS 321 in Park Slope.
- **Spot:** His district office at 1360 Fulton St, Suite 500, Bed-Stuy, in the Restoration Plaza block at Fulton and New York Aves (about 40.6800, -73.9470). I didn't confirm that 1360 is inside the Restoration complex; the block itself is right.
- **Facts:** He was elected in 2021 at 23, the youngest Council member ever and NYC's first Gen-Z elected official. He came out of the 2020 Black Lives Matter protests, where he co-founded Warriors in the Garden. In Feb 2026 he launched a campaign against deed theft (fraud that takes a homeowner's house, often through shell LLCs). He also declared "Lil' Kim Day" (see FACTS-cameos.md).
- **Quote:** "No one should be forced out while the courts are still determining whether a home was stolen." (Council press release, Feb 2 2026)
- **Quote:** "Deed theft is one of the most pervasive and least discussed housing crises in New York," (same release)
- **Pocket Civics:** Same job as Hanif: one of 51 votes on city laws and the budget. He can also hold hearings and push the state, which is where deed-theft law actually gets written.
- **Flag:** He was arrested twice in 2026: on Apr 22 at a Bed-Stuy anti-eviction protest, and in September with 100+ others at a protest before Netanyahu's UN speech. He filed to challenge Jeffries in Nov 2025, then dropped out within weeks without DSA's backing. Don't put him next to Jeffries as friends. Better yet, keep them apart.

**Letitia James: CAUTION, active federal case**
- **Title:** New York State Attorney General. She won a third term on Nov 3 2026 with about 60% against Saritha Komatireddy.
- **Tie:** She was raised in Park Slope and represented Fort Greene, Clinton Hill and parts of Prospect Heights, Crown Heights and Bed-Stuy on the Council (District 35, 2004–2013). She was then Public Advocate (2014–2018).
- **Spot:** The AG's Brooklyn Regional Office, 55 Hanson Pl, Suite 1080, Fort Greene (about 40.6854, -73.9755). It's a real public office where people bring consumer and elder-abuse complaints. Jeffries' Central Brooklyn district office is in the same building (Suite 603).
- **Facts:** She is the first woman and the first Black person elected NY Attorney General, and the first Black woman to hold statewide office in New York. Her office sued the NRA, won a $4.3M judgment against Wayne LaPierre, and brought the Trump civil fraud case. On Nov 13 2026 she opened an investigation into a deadly Brooklyn apartment building collapse.
- **Quote:** "Everyone deserves a safe place to call home." (AG press release, Nov 23 2026)
- **Quote:** "I encourage all New Yorkers to follow my office's tips to ensure their donations are effective." (AG release on the Brooklyn building collapse, Nov 13 2026, read on the EIN Presswire repost, not ag.ny.gov)
- **Pocket Civics:** The Attorney General is the state's lawyer, "the people's lawyer." She sues companies that cheat you, defends tenants and workers, and challenges the federal government in court. Most street crime is handled by the county District Attorney, not her.
- **Flag:** She was federally indicted in Oct 2025 on mortgage fraud. A judge dismissed the case in Nov 2025 (the prosecutor had been unlawfully appointed), and two grand juries refused to re-indict her in Dec 2025. DOJ appealed to the Fourth Circuit on Feb 9 2026. I found no ruling, but I didn't confirm anything after that. Her Trump fraud case is also still on appeal. Both make her highly partisan. Stick to consumer protection and the building collapse.

**Hakeem Jeffries: OK (facts only, no politics)**
- **Title:** House Democratic Leader (Minority Leader) and Speaker-designate. House Democrats nominated him for Speaker 251–11 on Nov 18 2026, and the full House votes on Jan 3. If elected, he will be the first Black Speaker. He won NY-8 unopposed in the June 23 primary and won the general with about 80%.
- **Tie:** He was raised in Crown Heights and is a lifelong member of Cornerstone Baptist Church in Bed-Stuy. NY-8 covers Bed-Stuy, Brownsville, East New York, Canarsie, Marine Park and Gravesend.
- **Spot:** Midwood High School, 2839 Bedford Ave, class of 1988 (about 40.6337, -73.9530). Alternatives: his Central Brooklyn office at 55 Hanson Pl (shared with James), or his South Brooklyn office at 445 Neptune Ave, Coney Island.
- **Facts:** In Nov 2022 he became the first African American to lead a party caucus in either chamber of Congress. He served in the NY State Assembly (57th district) from 2007 to 2012 before Congress (2013–). Shirley Chisholm was his congresswoman as a child.
- **Quote:** "I was raised in a working-class neighborhood in Crown Heights" (amNY, Nov 18 2026)
- **Quote:** "During the first 12 years of my life, my congresswoman, though I never met her, was Shirley Chisholm" (same article)
- **Pocket Civics:** The Speaker runs the House. The Speaker decides which bills get a vote and is second in line for the presidency, after the Vice President. Until January he leads the minority party.
- **Flag:** He is about to be the most powerful Democrat in Washington, so anything beyond biography reads as partisan. His Nov 18 remarks include jabs at "MAGA extremism"; I left those out on purpose.

**Chuck Schumer: CAUTION, leadership in flux**
- **Title:** Senate Democratic Leader (Minority Leader). Democrats won a 56-seat majority on Nov 3 2026. Sen. Chris Van Hollen announced on Nov 14 that he is challenging Schumer for Majority Leader, and the caucus votes in December (I didn't find the exact date). He turns 76 today, Nov 23.
- **Tie:** Born in Midwood and went through Brooklyn public schools. He represented Brooklyn in the State Assembly (1975–1980) and the House (1981–1999), and has been a senator since 1999.
- **Spot:** James Madison High School, 3787 Bedford Ave, where he was valedictorian in 1967 (about 40.6097, -73.9480). This is the same pin as the Bernie Sanders cameo, and Ruth Bader Ginsburg went there too. That could be a fun "three alumni" moment if you want one.
- **Facts:** In 2021 he became the first Jewish Senate leader in U.S. history and the first New Yorker to lead the Senate. He created the "Schumer box," the standard credit-card rate disclosure (1989). He is the longest-serving U.S. senator from New York.
- **Quote:** "The education I got at Madison and growing up in Brooklyn gave me something those kids didn't have. Street smarts," (NY1, Mar 20 2015, visiting Madison)
- **Quote:** "When I was a little kid, I'd sneak through holes in the fence so we could play baseball here," (same NY1 piece)
- **Pocket Civics:** The Senate Majority Leader decides which bills and which nominees, like judges and cabinet members, get a vote on the Senate floor. A senator's whole job is writing and passing federal law and approving those nominees.
- **Flag:** His leadership is openly contested right now, and he could lose the December vote. Write his cameo around Madison and Brooklyn so it survives either outcome. The quotes are from 2015; I found no 2026 Brooklyn quote from him.
