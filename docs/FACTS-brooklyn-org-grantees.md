# Fact sheet: Brooklyn Org grantees and issue areas

Researched Nov 21 2026. "(unverified)" marks items without a confirming source.

Deroy, here's the fact sheet. Four things to know first:

- Neighbors in Action is confirmed as a 2024 Spark winner.
- Brooklyn Org has no grantee called "Neighborhood Collective." The real program is the **Neighborhood Collective Impact Project**.
- Most source pages don't name a neighborhood. Where I add one, it's marked "(unverified)".
- The game's Bridge mission puts you on a "resident advisory council," and that matches something real. Brooklyn Org has a 12-member Grantmaking Advisory Council.

## 1. Brooklyn Org issue areas, funds and programs
- **12 issue areas:** Families, Youth, Immigrants, Older Adults, Civil Rights, Justice Reform, Civic Engagement, Environmental Justice, Housing, Health & Well-Being, Jobs & Economic Opportunity, Arts & Culture. https://brooklyn.org/all-issues/
- **Spark Prize:** started 2016. Five nonprofits a year get $100K "no strings attached," presented by NewYork-Presbyterian Brooklyn Methodist. Over $5.4M awarded so far. Finalists for the 10th prize were announced Oct 8 2026, but the names didn't come through when I fetched the page. https://brooklyn.org/sparkprize/ · https://www.brooklynpaper.com/brooklyn-org-celebrates-2026-spark-prize-winners/
- **Brooklyn Backs Brooklyn:** launched July 2025 in response to federal cuts and immigration enforcement. The goal is $5M and 25% more community-led grantmaking. Round 1 (Jan 2026) gave 12 nonprofits $40K each. https://brooklyn.org/article/brooklyn-backs-brooklyn-campaign-funds-12-nonprofits-to-protect-families-and-rights/
- **Operating-support grant cycles:** Dec 2025, 16 nonprofits, $1.5M multi-year. Spring 2026, 15 nonprofits, over $1.5M. https://brooklyn.org/article/brooklyn-org-funds-16-nonprofits-as-government-funding-cuts-impact-services-to-communities/ · https://brooklyn.org/article/brooklyn-org-awards-more-than-1-5-million-in-new-grants-across-brooklyn/
- **Older-adults funding:** seeded with $10M from two field-of-interest funds, CABS Community Foundation and the Fund for the Health & Integrity of Seniors. https://brooklyn.org/issue/older-adults/
- **Other:** Sunset Park Fund, Donor Advised Funds, Changemakers Ball, Listening Tour. https://brooklyn.org/

## 3. Issue areas the game doesn't cover yet
- **Older adults (beyond heat):** GRIOT Circle serves 400+ older adults, mostly Black, African American and Caribbean American (Spark 2018; 3-year grant). Volunteers of Legal Service gives low-income older adults free legal planning (Dec 2025). https://brooklyn.org/griot-circle/ · https://brooklyn.org/article/brooklyn-org-funds-16-nonprofits-as-government-funding-cuts-impact-services-to-communities/
- **Domestic and gender-based violence:** Asiyah Women's Center was NYC's first emergency shelter for survivors from Arab, Middle Eastern, Muslim and South Asian (AMEMSA) and BIPOC communities (Spark 2026). Sakhi for South Asian Survivors (Spring 2026). https://brooklyn.org/article/brooklyn-paper-2026-spark-prize-spotlight-asiyah-womens-center-supports-survivors-of-intimate-partner-violence/
- **Young men and violence prevention:** The B.R.O. Experience Foundation offers healing and mentorship for Black and Brown boys (Spark 2026). We Build the Block runs a drop-in space for young men affected by gun violence (Dec 2025). KAVI treats violence as a public-health issue (Spark 2023). https://www.brooklynpaper.com/brooklyn-org-spark-prize-2026-b-r-o-experience-foundation/
- **Environmental justice:** UPROSE organizes on climate justice in Sunset Park (Dec 2025). RETI Center does community solar and green-jobs training (Spring 2026). https://brooklyn.org/article/brooklyn-org-awards-more-than-1-5-million-in-new-grants-across-brooklyn/
- **LGBTQ+ and the arts:** Black Trans Femmes in the Arts (Spark 2026). QARAVAN supports LGBTQ+ immigrants (Dec 2025). https://brooklyn.org/article/brooklyn-org-reveals-winners-of-the-2026-spark-prize-honoring-five-trailblazing-nonprofits/
- **Digital divide:** TechFIN, Technology for Families in Need (Spark 2025). https://www.bkreader.com/non-profit-community/brooklyn-org-honors-5-trailblazing-nonprofits-for-2025-spark-prize-10046135
- **Early childhood and families:** Power of Two works on the first 1,000 days and intergenerational trauma (Spark 2024; Brooklyn Backs Brooklyn 2026). https://brooklyn.org/article/brooklyn-backs-brooklyn-campaign-funds-12-nonprofits-to-protect-families-and-rights/
- **Veterans:** Black Veterans for Social Justice (Dec 2025). Same Dec 2025 source.
- **Workers:** Workers Justice Project (Spark 2023). Street Vendor Project (Apr 2025 round). https://brooklyn.org/sparkprize/

## 4. Programs that put residents in the room
- **Grantmaking Advisory Council:** 12 Brooklynites, 2-year terms. They review requests, join staff on site visits and help pick grantees. Council-backed grants are announced twice a year. https://brooklyn.org/article/the-new-yorkers-helping-us-move-millions-to-brooklyns-communities/ · https://brooklyn.org/volunteer/
- **History (unconfirmed):** a search summary credited Brooklyn Org with starting participatory grantmaking in 2015 and moving all strategic grantmaking to councils in 2021, the first community foundation to do so. I couldn't confirm that on a page I read. It likely comes from the Jan 2026 white paper. https://brooklyn.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Brooklyn-Org-White-Paper-The-Foundation-Of-Our-Future_January-2026.pdf
- **Spark Prize Committee:** community members review applications, pick 20 finalists, interview them and choose five. https://brooklyn.org/volunteer/
- **Neighborhood Collective Impact Project:** launched 2024, grew out of the Listening Tour. 2026 round (Jul 2026): three $25K grants, each to a partnership of nonprofits:
  - Anti-violence workshops in East Flatbush (Center for Anti-Violence Education with Flanbwayan)
  - Bed-Stuy University (Magnolia Tree Earth Center with Liminal)
  - D13 Teach Fabulous!, LGBTQ+ family engagement (MJR Love with PFLAG NYC)

  https://www.bkreader.com/non-profit-community/brooklyn-org-gives-neighborhood-projects-a-boost-12528305
- **Listening Tour:** held every year 2022–2024 and in 2026. The 2026 tour covered Bay Ridge/Dyker Heights, Bed-Stuy/Bushwick, Canarsie, Crown Heights, Flatbush/Midwood, Marine Park and a youth session. In 2025 it was replaced by The People's Pulse survey (3,000 NYC adults) and two forums. https://brooklyn.org/listening-tour/
- **Sunset Park Fund:** a resident Advisory Council sets the focus, reads applications and decides grants. Members should be long-term residents who don't lead a local nonprofit and can commit through Spring 2027. Money comes from 5% of South Brooklyn Marine Terminal rents, with Councilmember Avilés and NYCEDC as partners. Applications open Winter 2027. https://brooklyn.org/sunset-park-fund/
- **Microgrants:** I found no current resident microgrant program.
