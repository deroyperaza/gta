# Fact sheet: living Brooklyn icon cameos

Researched Nov 21 2026.

Seven of the twelve are fine for a cameo. Hold Jay-Z and Talib Kweli, and skip Michael Jordan. Lil' Kim and Bernie Sanders each need a decision from you. I checked every quote below against a source, and all are short and none are lyrics. Caveats: all lat/lng are my own approximations (check each pin on a map), and some quotes I saw only in a secondary source, as marked.

**Jay-Z: HOLD**
- **Tie:** Marcy Houses, Bed-Stuy. Stand on Marcy Ave at Myrtle, about 40.6950, -73.9495.
- **Facts:** He started the Shawn Carter Scholarship Foundation with his mother, Gloria, in 2003. The foundation holds a yearly toy giveaway at Marcy.
- **Quote:** "I was nine years old, the summer of 1978, and Marcy was my world." (*Decoded*, 2010, opening line)
- **Flag:** He is still in active litigation. A California appeals court upheld the dismissal of his suit against attorney Tony Buzbee on Sep 9, 2026, and refused a rehearing on Oct 1. His federal suit (SDNY 1:26-cv-02775) is still open. That fight grew out of a withdrawn rape lawsuit against him.
- https://www.courthousenews.com/appeals-court-has-99-problems-with-jay-zs-extortion-lawsuit-against-attorney/ · https://archive.org/stream/DecodedByJayZ/Decoded%20by%20Jay-Z_djvu.txt

**Spike Lee: OK**
- **Tie:** Fort Greene. His studio was in the old firehouse at 124 DeKalb Ave from 1985 to 2008 (about 40.6893, -73.9743); it is now on S. Elliott Pl.
- **Facts:** He made *Do the Right Thing* at that firehouse. In 2015 the city co-named a block of Stuyvesant Ave in Bed-Stuy "Do the Right Thing Way," the first time a work of art named a city street.
- **Quote:** "We had a big, old house right across the street from Fort Greene Park." (NPR *Fresh Air*, 2017)
- **Flag:** I couldn't confirm that a "Spike Lee Place" sign exists, so don't use one.
- https://freshairarchive.org/segments/31-years-later-spike-lee-puts-new-spin-shes-gotta-have-it · https://streeteasy.com/blog/spike-lee-nyc/amp/

**Barbra Streisand: OK, with care**
- **Tie:** Erasmus Hall HS, 911 Flatbush Ave (about 40.6505, -73.9573). As a child she lived at 365 Pulaski St; sources disagree on whether that is Bed-Stuy or Williamsburg.
- **Facts:** She graduated from Erasmus as an honors student at 16, in 1959. As a five-year-old she sang in her building's echoing hallway.
- **Quote:** "the last time that I sang solo here was on somebody's stoop on Pulaski Street." (*Back to Brooklyn*, Barclays, 2012; I saw it only in a secondary summary of the PBS special, not the transcript)
- **Flag:** She guards her privacy closely (the 2003 photo lawsuit behind "the Streisand effect"). Keep the cameo respectful and avoid caricature.
- https://booktrib.com/2023/12/18/my-name-is-barbra-streisand/ · https://watch.opb.org/video/barbra-streisand-back-to-brooklyn-rumtnq/

**Michael Jordan: SKIP**
- **Tie:** He was born at Cumberland Hospital in Fort Greene on Feb 17, 1963 (about 40.6950, -73.9770) and moved to Wilmington, NC as a small child.
- **Quote:** none.
- **Flag:** He sues over unauthorized use of his name. A jury awarded him $8.9M against Dominick's in 2015, and he settled with Jewel-Osco. His Brooklyn tie is also only his birthplace.
- https://chicago.suntimes.com/2016/6/24/18408081/jurors-award-michael-jordan-8-9-million-in-dominick-s-lawsuit

**Rosie Perez: OK**
- **Tie:** Bushwick. Maria Hernandez Park is a good public spot (about 40.7034, -73.9235); that spot is my pick, not a documented link.
- **Facts:** She co-founded Urban Arts Partnership, which brings arts education to low-income NYC schools. She earned an Oscar nomination for *Fearless* (1993); I didn't re-verify that this session.
- **Quote:** "The arts opened me up, it really did." (NBC Latino, 2017)
- https://www.nbcnews.com/news/latino/rosie-perez-urban-arts-continue-fight-arts-education-underprivileged-students-n733381

**Yasiin Bey: OK**
- **Tie:** Bed-Stuy, Roosevelt Houses at DeKalb and Lewis Aves (about 40.6939, -73.9339).
- **Facts:** He has acted and rapped since age 9. With Talib Kweli he bought Nkiru Books, one of Brooklyn's first Black bookstores (sources give 1997 to 2000).
- **Quote:** "My first home was Marcy Projects. My second home was Roosevelt Projects." (Kalamu interview, 2014)
- **Flag:** He has lived in Cape Town and had a 2016 visa dispute in South Africa. Neither is a cameo problem.
- https://kalamu.com/neogriot/2014/03/22/interview-yasiin-bey-aka-mos-def-from-brooklyn-to-bo-kaap/ · https://patch.com/new-york/bed-stuy/today-s-pride-of-bed-stuy-yasiin-bey-aka-mos-def-b95eaa51

**Talib Kweli: HOLD**
- **Tie:** Nkiru Books, 76 St. Marks Ave, Park Slope (about 40.6810, -73.9760).
- **Facts:** His parents were educators. Nkiru was founded in 1976, and he worked there from 1994 before buying it.
- **Quote:** "'Nkiru' means 'the best is yet to come'" (Village Voice)
- **Flag:** He was accused of a 2020 harassment campaign against Maya Moody. She says she is still being harassed and has a court date in February. He also has a past accusation from the singer Res. Not suitable for kids right now.
- https://www.villagevoice.com/brooklyns-nkiru-books-rises-up-once-more-with-help-from-talib-kweli/ · https://www.complex.com/music/a/alex-ocho/maya-moody-talib-kweli-court-february

**Chris Rock: OK, location is soft**
- **Tie:** Bed-Stuy. I found no documented spot for him; Decatur St comes from his brother Tony's page. If you use it, label it generic (about 40.6830, -73.9300).
- **Facts:** At 7 he was bused from Bed-Stuy to a school in Gerritsen Beach. *Everybody Hates Chris* is based on his childhood.
- **Quote:** "I was bused from Bed-Stuy to Gerritsen Beach" (The Root)
- **Flag:** His stand-up is adult material.
- https://www.theroot.com/revealing-roots-chris-rock-uncovers-the-ancestral-source-of-his-drive

**Carmelo Anthony: OK, strong pick**
- **Tie:** Red Hook East Houses. His family lived at 79 Lorraine St until he was 8. The courts he renovated are near 62 Mill St (about 40.6770, -74.0050).
- **Facts:** His foundation and JPMorgan Chase refurbished the Red Hook courts in 2013. He won three Olympic golds; I didn't re-verify that this session.
- **Quote:** "Red Hook is where I started and where I've returned to begin my Courts 4 Kids efforts." (NYCHA press release, 2013)
- https://www.nyc.gov/site/nycha/about/press/pr-2013/nycha-carmelo-anthony-nycha-unveil.page

**Lil' Kim: CAUTION**
- **Tie:** Born in Bed-Stuy, 1974. I found no specific documented spot, so any pin is generic.
- **Facts:** Council Member Chi Ossé declared July 11 "Lil' Kim Day" in Brooklyn at her Kings Theatre show, Nov 2026. A citywide resolution (Res. 740) is still in committee.
- **Quote:** none verified.
- **Flag:** She has a 2005 perjury conviction and her catalog is explicit. Not litigation, but it matters for a kids' game.
- https://thegrio.com/2026/11/21/lil-kim-day-new-york-city-council-resolution/

**Bernie Sanders: CAUTION, active politician**
- **Tie:** James Madison HS, 3787 Bedford Ave (about 40.6097, -73.9480). He grew up at 1525 E. 26th St. Sources say Midwood, but he has said Flatbush himself.
- **Facts:** He went to PS 197, then James Madison (class of 1959), then a year at Brooklyn College. His family lived in a three-and-a-half-room rent-controlled apartment.
- **Quote:** "I spent the first 18 years of my life in Brooklyn" (Yahoo News; I saw it only in a search summary, so open the article to confirm)
- **Flag:** He is a sitting U.S. senator. Using him could read as partisan unless you balance it or stick to his school history only.
- https://www.yahoo.com/news/bernie-sanders-immigrant-brooklyn-roots-191215942.html · https://www.dnainfo.com/new-york/20160224/midwood/map-bernie-sanders-new-york/

**Mel Brooks: OK**
- **Tie:** Grew up in Williamsburg. I couldn't verify a home address, so the spot is generic (about 40.7090, -73.9570). My sources never mention Brownsville, so don't use it.
- **Facts:** He turned 100 on June 28, 2026. He created the "2000 Year Old Man" sketches with Carl Reiner.
- **Quote:** "I was born to make people laugh." (*Mel Brooks: The 99 Year Old Man!*, HBO, 2026)
- **Flag:** He was alive at his June 2026 birthday; I didn't check anything later. Confirm before you ship.
- https://www.nbcnews.com/pop-culture/celebrity/mel-brooks-turns-100-rcna352106

The cleanest seven are Carmelo, Rosie, Spike, Yasiin Bey, Mel Brooks, Chris Rock and Barbra Streisand.

## Added Nov 22 2026 (Deroy asked for the Mayor and the Borough President)

**Zohran Mamdani: OK (facts only, no politics)**
- **Tie:** Flatbush Avenue's new center bus lanes, Flatbush Ave at DeKalb, by the B41 stop (about 40.6901, -73.9819).
- **Facts:** Took office Jan 1 2026. On Nov 17 2026 the city finished 1.9 miles of protected center-running bus lanes on Flatbush with six concrete boarding islands; buses there averaged about 4 mph; 120,000 daily riders; B41, B67, B45, B103; BRT planned by 2030.
- **Quote:** "Flatbush Avenue is the foundation for the future of bus service in New York City." (NYC Mayor's Office release, Nov 17 2026)
- https://nyc.gov/mayors-office/news/2026/11/mayor-mamdani-delivers-faster-buses-on-flatbush-avenue--laying-g · https://www.nyc.gov/mayors-office/news/2026/01/mayor-zohran-mamdani-inaugural-address

**Antonio Reynoso: OK (facts only)**
- **Tie:** Brooklyn Borough Hall (about 40.6925, -73.9897).
- **Facts:** First Latino Brooklyn Borough President; born and raised in Los Sures (Williamsburg's Southside) to parents from the Dominican Republic; two terms on the City Council (District 34).
- **Quote:** "All roads lead to Brooklyn, and even when others try to turn us against each other, Brooklyn chooses belonging." (2026 State of the Borough, April 2026)
- https://www.brooklynbp.nyc.gov/brooklyn-borough-president-antonio-reynoso-celebrates-brooklyn-at-2026-state-of-the-borough-address/ · https://www.brooklynbp.nyc.gov/brooklyn-borough-president-antonio-reynoso-celebrates-inauguration-in-public-ceremony/
